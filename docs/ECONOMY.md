# Economy and tech evolution (Commander rules)

Commander rules give the economy and the tech tree more depth. You choose how far to climb, what to upgrade and
what to research, and each choice is a trade between three things:

- **eco:** more income later;
- **army:** more units now;
- **tech:** better units for the rest of the match.

Classic rules and the campaigns are pure Red Alert and have none of this.

## At a glance

| Stage | What you decide |
|---|---|
| **Early (Tier 1)** | Refineries and trucks, or an early army? Turbines or more power plants? Forward Mining Outposts, or a safe base? Bunkers for your riflemen? |
| **Mid (Tier 2)** | Climb to Tier 2, then choose: a Research Lab, the Ore Processor and Purifier, the Assembly Line, a Logistics Centre, or simply more tanks. |
| **Late (Tier 3)** | Level II research, the Armour Shop and superweapons. These are big investments that pay off in a long war. |

## Tech tiers

Select your **Construction Yard** to see its upgrades. A tier you have reached is yours for good: a new or rebuilt
yard shows it too. Locked cards in the build bar say which tier they need ("Requires T2").

| Tier | Upgrade | Cost | Time | Needs | Unlocks |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| T1 Foundation | (start) | | | | The Red Alert tech tree except the Tech Centres and superweapons, plus Mining Outposts and Bunkers |
| T2 Industrial | Yard: cranes and extra bays | $1,000 | 30 s | Refinery, Barracks | Research Lab, Logistics Centre, Ore Purifier / Smelter, Fortress Gate, Tech Centre (and its units), Tier 2 upgrades |
| T3 Command | Yard: command tower | $2,000 | 45 s | Tier 2, Radar Dome | Superweapons, Level II research, Armour Shop |

## Structure upgrades

Select a structure and click an upgrade in its panel. An upgrade is paid while it builds, like production. If you run
out of money it waits, and cancelling it refunds what you paid. Its module appears on the building. If the
structure is sold or destroyed, the upgrade is gone, and a structure an enemy engineer captures keeps its
upgrades for the new owner.

| Upgrade | On | Cost | Time | Tier | Effect |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Turbine | Power Plant, Advanced Power Plant | $300 | 15 s | 1 | +50% power from this plant |
| Ore Processor | Ore Refinery | $800 | 25 s | 2 | Ore unloaded here is worth 20% more, and trucks unload twice as fast |
| Training Ground | Barracks | $1,000 | 30 s | 2 | Infantry trained here arrive as Veterans |
| Assembly Line | War Factory (one per player) | $1,500 | 40 s | 2 | All vehicles build 25% faster |
| Armour Shop | War Factory (one per player) | $2,000 | 45 s | 3 | All your vehicles +10% health while you keep it |
| Reinforced | Any defence, Bunker, Gate | 40% of its price | 12 s | 2 | +30% health for that defence |
| Satellite Uplink | Radar Dome (one per player) | $1,200 | 30 s | 2 | The dome sees 6 cells further; GPS, spy plane, paratroopers and parabombs recharge 25% faster |

## Research

Build a **Research Lab** (Tier 2, $1,200) and select it to open the research tree. Each lab runs one project at a
time, so a second lab doubles your research speed. Research applies to your whole army, including the units you
already have. Every line has two levels, and Level II needs Tier 3.

| Faction | Research | Level I | Level II |
|---|---|---|---|
| Allies | **Composite Armour** | Vehicles take 8% less damage ($1,500) | 15% less ($2,500) |
| Allies | **Precision Optics** | Vehicle and defence weapons reach 6% further ($1,500) | 12% further ($2,500) |
| Allies | **Rapid Deployment** | Units build 15% faster ($1,200) | Structures, upgrades and research also build 25% faster ($2,000) |
| Allies | **Chrono Logistics** | Full Ore Trucks more than 10 cells from a refinery chronoshift next to it, each once every 45 s ($1,500) | The Chronosphere and Chrono Tanks recharge 30% faster ($2,000) |
| Soviet | **Reactive Armour** | Heavy, Mammoth, Tesla and M.A.D. Tanks +15% health ($1,500) | +30% ($2,500) |
| Soviet | **Tesla Amplifiers** | Tesla weapons deal 15% more damage ($1,200) | Tesla Coils reach 15% further and drain 40% less power ($2,000) |
| Soviet | **Conscription** | Infantry cost 15% less ($1,000) | 25% less, and they train 25% faster ($2,000) |
| Soviet | **Heavy Industry** | Vehicles cost 10% less ($1,500) | 15% less, and they build 15% faster ($2,500) |
| Both | **Improved Harvesters** | Ore Trucks carry 25% more and take 25% less damage ($1,000) | They carry 50% more ($1,800) |
| Both | **Field Medicine** | Infantry heal 1.5% of their health per second after 8 s out of combat ($1,000) | Vehicles heal 1% per second too ($1,800) |

**Doctrine synergy.** The research or upgrade that fits your doctrine costs 25% less, and the tree marks it:

| Doctrine | Cheaper |
|---|---|
| Armoured Fist | Reactive Armour |
| Human Wave | Conscription |
| Tesla Doctrine | Tesla Amplifiers |
| Iron Rain | Heavy Industry |
| Blitzkrieg | Rapid Deployment |
| Fortress Europe | Reinforced (defence upgrade) |
| Precision Strike | Precision Optics |
| Shadow Network | Chrono Logistics |

Doctrine bonuses and research stack. Human Wave plus Conscription II makes infantry very cheap. Tesla Doctrine's
power-hungry coils love Amplifiers II.

## New structures

| Structure | Tier | Cost | Power | What it does |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Mining Outpost** | 1 | $800 | −10 | A small refinery with one dock and storage for $500. It needs a refinery first. You can place it next to any ore field you can see right now (within 4 cells of the ore), however far from your base, but not within 8 cells of an enemy structure. Ore Trucks unload at the nearest dock. Only defences can be built next to an outpost. |
| **Bunker** | 1 | $600 | 0 | Concrete, 900 health. Up to 4 armed infantry go inside: select them and right-click the bunker. They fire from its walls (about a cell further than in the open) and can't be hurt. Select the bunker and press **Evacuate** (D) to bring them out. They also come out if the bunker is sold, captured or destroyed, and lose 30% of their health if it is destroyed. |
| **Research Lab** | 2 | $1,200 | −60 | Researches the upgrades above. |
| **Ore Purifier** (Allies) / **Ore Smelter** (Soviet) | 2 | $1,500 | −75 | All ore you deliver is worth 25% more (Purifier) or 40% more (Smelter: the Soviet haul makes up for the Allied late game). One is enough. It does nothing while your power is low. |
| **Logistics Centre** | 2 | $1,500 | −40 | All units cost 10% less. It repairs your vehicles (2% per second) and structures (0.5% per second) within 6 cells for free. One is enough for the discount. |
| **Fortress Gate** | 2 | $200 | 0 | A 1-cell gate placed like a wall: in or near a wall line. It opens for your team's units, and stays shut while an enemy is next to it. Your units plan their routes through it; enemies have to break it down. |

## Reading your economy

The economy readout (the **$/min** chip next to your credits) shows:
- your income per minute over the last minute;
- your power;
- the ore still in the fields near your docks, with how many truck loads that is;
- your Ore Truck count (with idle trucks flagged).

Select a refinery or outpost to see the ore left around it. Gems are still worth twice as much as ore.

## Harvesters

Under Commander rules, Ore Trucks:
- skip ore fields where they can see enemy fighters, and come back to them once the fields are clear;
- unload at the nearest free dock: a refinery or an outpost;
- drive through your own Fortress Gates.

There is no unit upkeep, so a big army costs only what you paid to build it.

## How the AI plays it

The AI uses the same systems. Its personality and difficulty decide how:
- **Aggressive** commanders tech late and buy army-wide upgrades: the Assembly Line and the Logistics Centre.
- **Tycoons** go economy first: Processors, a Purifier or Smelter, Mining Outposts and Improved Harvesters.
- **Turtles** reinforce their defences and man their bunkers.
- **Tech-minded** commanders climb the tiers early and research more.
- **Easier AIs** start later and run fewer projects at once.
